Quorum AutoPay is in pilot

In April I told you we were building payments directly into the DMS and aiming for a summer launch. We are piloting it now.

Here is what changes at the counter. Your staff no longer key the amount into the terminal. The DMS sends the amount to the terminal and collects the payment reference ID back automatically, so what was typed and what was charged can never drift apart. Then it goes further: we automate the payout journals and the reconciliation process, which is the part your accounting office will care about more than anyone.

If you want to be in the next group of pilot stores, let me know. I will get you connected with the right folks for a demo and get you on the list, which is filling up faster than I expected.

Sales CRM

The Sales AI Assistant is in pilot (and it is free)

In April I said we were starting conversations with customers about where AI could help sales staff. It is now a real product, in pilot as of mid August.

From the Traffic Log, one click has the assistant read the customer's activity and history and hand your salesperson a Lead Intent Score, a suggested next message, call talking points and a recommended next action. Instead of digging through notes to work out where a lead stands, they get a head start on every follow-up. If you want in on the pilot, reply to this email. There is no charge.

Link to Traffic

When a customer's journey ends up split across two traffic records (usually after a merge or a duplicate created at lead intake), the contact stage goes wrong, the visit history is incomplete and notes live on a record nobody is looking at. Link to Traffic is a new manager-level tool on the Traffic Log History tab that repairs those broken chains in a single action: pick the two logs, set parent and child, assign the role, confirm.

The Pending Guest workflow got a rethink

This is a set of changes aimed squarely at duplicate customer records. Pending guest details now show on the Traffic Log row, adding a guest starts with a customer search instead of a blank form, double-clicking a pending guest opens a pre-seeded search (or the Process Lead tab if it came from an internet lead), and the buttons are now labelled Log First Visit, Log Be-Back and Log New Traffic so it is obvious what each one does. Small changes individually, but together they should meaningfully cut down on the duplicates your managers are cleaning up.

Odds and ends worth knowing

Overdue sales appointments no longer drop off the Traffic Log (the calendar icon turns red instead), Create Another on a worksheet now adds a second worksheet to the same traffic rather than creating new traffic, texting templates now support Sales Leads, and there is a new SalesPersonCell merge field for email and text templates.

Service CRM & MORI

MORI keeps getting better

Appointment confirmation emails. Turn on Service Appt Email Confirmation (Internal) in MORI settings, add the emails you want notified, and you will know every time MORI books an appointment.

Pooled minutes for dealer groups. Groups with three or more stores on MORI now share one pool of minutes, and overages only apply once the pool is exceeded.

Call forwarding from your queues. If you use DealerMine Telephony, you can now forward a call to MORI once a queue has looped a set number of times, instead of dropping the customer into voicemail.

Smarter web leads. Leads MORI emails into DealerMine are now parsed automatically, so the customer name populates and, where the name and phone clearly match, the lead links itself to the right customer profile.

Receptionist or overflow. You can now tell MORI which role it is playing (front line reception, or overflow and after hours only), and it adjusts how and when it transfers calls versus sends an email.

A major user interface upgrade

With 9.8.4 we completed a significant upgrade to the framework underneath the DealerMine interface. It sets us up for a more modern and more responsive product going forward.

Telephony on its own infrastructure

We moved telephony traffic off the shared Common database onto a dedicated Telephony server. That improves reliability and performance for telephony and for everything else that used to compete with it. We also resolved the intermittent inbound and outbound call failures some of you experienced, and fixed voice recordings that could play back from the wrong store after our move to AWS.

A solid run of DMS integration fixes

A fair bit of work went into the other DMS integrations this cycle: Tekion phone numbers no longer get cleared when an appointment is created, Tekion open ROs no longer duplicate on the appointment schedule and inflate your shop hours, voided ROs now clear properly from customer profiles, CDK makes and models populate again, and duplicate op codes stay on the appointment where they belong.

User management is done, and it is yours now

We finished the User Management overhaul. Anyone with Desk Settings access can now create and deactivate users for their rooftop directly in Autovance, without calling support. New users get an onboarding email automatically. Users tied to multiple rooftops still need us, but you can raise that ticket right from the interface.

Selective F&I updates to the DMS

Built with the Quorum DMS team, this lets Autovance send only the F&I related updates (fees, F&I products, extended warranties, insurance products and vehicle pricing) across to the DMS. Changes your team made directly on the DMS worksheet stay intact, while the F&I side stays current.

First Payment Date in Deal Info

Autovance has always calculated the first payment date from the contract date and payment schedule. Now it shows it to you, right in the Deal Information section.

First Canadian is coming

The foundation work for the First Canadian Financial Group integration is in place. When it lands, their full suite of protection products, with rates and options, will display directly in your deal instead of you switching between systems. This is the first of several F&I provider integrations we are building across North America.

Freight and PDI charges were pushing to PBS as $0 and forcing your F&I managers to correct them on every deal. That is fixed. Accessible Accessories now supports a new API endpoint that lets partners pull full quote details using either a quote number or a VIN.
