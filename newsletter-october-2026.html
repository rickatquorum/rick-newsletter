SPOTLIGHT

Quorum Auto Pay is live, and Pay by Link is next.

One payment provider, one bank deposit, no chasing links

Auto Pay is out of pilot and live with customers, and as of this release your staff can also take a card over the phone right from the same window. Next up is Pay by Link, the biggest feature we have built for Auto Pay yet.

From a Work Order, Parts Sale or Cash Transaction, your staff send the customer a secure payment link through Communicator by email or text. The customer pays from their phone, and the payment posts directly to the transaction and lands in the bank with the rest of your Auto Pay payments. No separate payment provider, no generating links by hand and no checking back to see if they were paid.

QAnalytics 2.0 is live

QAnalytics 2.0 is live and in use across our customers. We are working closely with customers to make sure this is an outstanding experience. The two features we are most excited about:

Build your own dashboards. Click + Dashboard, search for the charts you want and drag them onto the canvas. Add an OEM/RT filter and a date range so the numbers reflect your store and period.

Schedule delivery to anyone. Under Settings > Alerts & Reports, pick a dashboard, set the schedule and add your recipients. Monday morning numbers land in your managers' inboxes without anyone pulling them.

Service CRM & MORI

MORI is ready for tire season

MORI adoption has not slowed down, and the biggest growth right now is inside our own BDC. The change every service manager should know about is Booking Days Out: tell MORI not to offer times inside a set number of days, for every appointment or for specific op codes. Set your tire swap op codes a few days out and your team has time to pull tires from storage before the customer arrives.

The settings screen arrives with DealerMine 9.8.7 in November. Until then, support can set it up for you (and if you already had a custom instruction for this, it has been applied).

MORI also moved to a newer model built for voice agents, can now book by confirming the vehicle when the name on file does not match, picks the right op code when a customer only wants part of a package, and no longer books past closing.

A heads up on ROI reports: duplicate ROs now count once, and only appointments booked after a campaign went out are included. Your numbers may come in lower than you are used to, but they are much more accurate.

The right worksheet, everywhere

When a traffic log has more than one worksheet, your salesperson can now pick the active one and it stays selected. That vehicle shows consistently across the traffic log, mobile app, follow ups, reports and customer emails, and carries over when a visit becomes a First Visit or Be-Back.

Cleaner appointment and activity tracking

Appointment follow ups no longer default to Show, so your team has to record the real outcome. Showroom History now shows the full activity for internet leads (just like walk-ins) and the correct visit date on linked traffic logs. And customers can confirm their sales appointments online again without an error.

Odds and ends worth knowing

Fetch has a new Contact Stage filter for building campaigns, KO address search only suggests real Canadian and US street addresses, the Web Leads badge only counts leads that need action, and the Up List shows available salespeople first again.
